Frequently Asked Questions

How much money should I save before maternity leave?

Financial planners recommend calculating your net income deficit during unpaid or partially paid leave plus upfront hospital and nursery expenses, typically creating a cash reserve of 3 to 6 months of living expenses.

How do Short-Term Disability and Paid Family Leave combine for maternity leave?

In many states, Short-Term Disability covers 6 to 8 weeks of physical recovery for the birthing parent, followed by state Paid Family Leave or company parental leave for bonding.

Can both parents take paid leave for the same baby?

Yes. Most state paid leave programs and employer policies grant both parents individual allotments of leave (typically 8 to 12 weeks each) that can be taken concurrently or consecutively.
